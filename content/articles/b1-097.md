In Japan, many people love to visit a hot spring, or onsen. It is a bath filled with natural hot water from under the ground. Last winter, I went to one in the mountains for the first time, and I will never forget it.

I travelled with my mother. It was snowing when we arrived, and the air was very cold. The small hotel was warm and quiet. A woman in a kimono welcomed us and gave us cups of green tea. Our room had a floor of soft mats, and we slept on futons.

Before going into the bath, I learned some rules from my mother. First, you must wash your body carefully at the washing place. You sit on a small stool and use soap and water. Only when you are clean can you enter the hot water. You must not put a towel into the bath. You can leave it on your head or on the side.

We chose an outdoor bath. I stepped out into the cold air, and then I slowly put my feet into the water. It was very hot at first, but soon it felt wonderful. Snow fell softly on my face while my body was warm. I could see the dark mountains and many stars. For a long time, neither of us spoke.

Hot spring water often has minerals, which many people believe are good for the skin and for tired muscles. I do not know if that is true. But I felt completely relaxed.

That night, we ate a big dinner with many small dishes. Then we went to sleep early. I am planning another trip this year.
