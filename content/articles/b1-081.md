You do not need a garden to grow food. A small balcony is enough. Tomatoes are a good plant to start with, and they taste much better when you grow them yourself.

First, choose a large pot. Tomato roots need space, so the pot should be at least as deep as your forearm. Make sure it has holes in the bottom. Water must be able to leave, or the roots will rot.

Next, buy a few small plants from a garden shop. It is easier than growing from seeds. Pick plants with strong green stems and no yellow leaves.

Fill the pot with good soil. Make a hole and put the plant in so that the soil covers the lower part of the stem. This helps the plant grow more roots. Then press the soil gently and give it a lot of water.

Put the pot in a place with plenty of sun. Tomatoes need about six hours of direct light every day. If your balcony is shady, they may not grow well.

Tomato plants grow tall, so they need support. Push a long stick into the soil next to the plant. Use soft string to tie the stem to the stick. Do not tie it too tightly.

Water the plant every day in hot weather. Pour the water at the base, not on the leaves. Once a week, add a little plant food to the water.

After two or three months, you will see small green fruit. Wait until they turn completely red. Then pick one, wash it, and eat it right away. You will not forget the taste.
