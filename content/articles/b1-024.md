A job interview can feel scary. You sit in a room with people you do not know, and they ask you questions about yourself. But if you prepare well, you can feel much calmer. Here are some simple steps.

First, learn about the company. Read its website, look at what it makes or sells, and find out how big it is. If you can, talk to someone who works there. When the interviewer asks, "Why do you want this job?", you will have a good answer, and it will show that you are really interested.

Second, read the job advertisement again. Think about what the company needs, and then think about what you can offer. Write down three or four things that you do well, and find a short story for each one. For example, if the job needs a good team player, you can tell about a time when you helped your group finish a hard task.

Third, practise your answers. Many interviews have the same questions, such as "Tell me about yourself". You do not have to learn the answers word for word. But if you say them aloud, with a friend or in front of a mirror, they will sound more natural on the day.

Fourth, think of some questions to ask. At the end, the interviewer usually says, "Do you have any questions?" Do not say no. You can ask about the team or about a normal day in the job. This shows that you are serious.

Fifth, plan the day. Choose your clothes the night before, and make sure they are clean and comfortable. Check the time and place, and plan to arrive ten minutes early.

Finally, relax. Take a deep breath, smile, and listen carefully before you answer. If you make a small mistake, do not worry. The interviewers are people too, and many of them remember how nervous they felt at their first interview.

Every interview makes the next one easier.
