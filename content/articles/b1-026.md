I am not a morning person, but over the years I have made a routine that helps me start the day well.

My alarm rings at six thirty. At first, I do not want to get up. I lie in bed for two minutes and think about something nice, such as a coffee, a holiday, or a good film. Then I stand up quickly. If I wait too long, I fall asleep again.

The first thing I do is open the window and drink a big glass of water. After a night of sleep, my body needs it. Then I do some easy exercise for ten minutes. I touch my toes, move my arms, and turn my head slowly. It is not hard, but it makes me feel awake.

After that, I take a shower. I use warm water at first, and then, for ten seconds, cold water. It is not nice, but it is a very good way to wake up.

Breakfast is my favourite part. I eat bread with cheese, an egg, and some fruit. I sit at the table and eat slowly. I do not look at my phone. Instead, I listen to the radio or look out of the window. This is the only quiet time in my day, and I enjoy it very much.

Before I leave, I write down three things that I want to do that day. If I do these three things, I feel good in the evening, even if the day was busy.

Then I take my bag, say goodbye to my cat, and go to the bus stop. I leave the house at ten to eight, so I never need to run.

Of course, some mornings are bad. Sometimes I sleep late, and I must do everything fast. But when I have my routine, the whole day goes better. My advice is simple: find a few small things that make you happy, and do them every morning.
