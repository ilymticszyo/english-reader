In a busy city, we see buildings, roads, and cars everywhere. But there is another thing that makes life in a city better: trees. A tree in the street is not only beautiful. It does a lot of useful work.

First, trees clean the air. Cars and factories fill the air with dirt and gases. The leaves of a tree catch small pieces of dirt, and the tree gives out the oxygen that we need to breathe.

Second, trees keep the city cooler. In summer, buildings and roads become very hot, because they take in the heat of the sun. A tree gives shade, and water from its leaves makes the air cooler, too. On a hot day, the temperature under a tree can be several degrees lower than in the sun. Some cities plant trees along the streets to help people during very hot weather.

Third, trees help when it rains. Hard ground cannot take in water, so after a storm, the water can fill the streets. The roots of a tree drink some of the rain, and the leaves hold a part of it before it reaches the ground. This can help to stop floods.

Trees are also good for our health and our mood. Studies show that people who live near green places have less stress, and that patients in hospitals get better a little faster when they can see trees from their windows. Even a short walk in a park can make us feel calmer.

But trees in cities have a hard life. They have little space for their roots, and many die young.

You can help, too. Plant a tree if you have a garden, or look after the trees in your street.
