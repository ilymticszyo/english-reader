When I was nine years old, my parents bought me a piano. I did not want it. I wanted to play football with my friends. For the first year, I hated my lessons, and I often cried. Now I am twenty-five, and I am very glad that my parents did not let me stop.

Many people think that learning an instrument is only for children. This is not true. You can start at any age, and there are many good reasons to do it.

First, music is good for your brain. When you play an instrument, you use your eyes, your ears, and your hands at the same time. Scientists say that this makes the brain stronger. Children who learn music often do better at school. For older people, it can help to keep the mind active.

Second, it teaches patience. You cannot play well after one day. You must practise slowly, again and again, even when it is boring. But then, one day, a difficult part suddenly becomes easy. That feeling is wonderful, and it teaches you that hard work brings results. This is a lesson that is useful in every part of life.

Third, music gives you a way to express your feelings. Sometimes, when I am sad or angry, I cannot find the words. But I can sit at the piano and play. After half an hour, I feel better.

Last, music brings people together. You can play in a band, in a school group, or just with friends at a party. Some of my best friends are people that I met through music.

If you want to start, here is some advice. Choose an instrument that you really like to listen to. Practise a little every day, even for only fifteen minutes, because short and regular practice is better than long practice once a week. Most important, enjoy it.

I am not a famous player, and I do not need to be. Music is a friend that I will have for my whole life.
