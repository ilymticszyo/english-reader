When our plane landed, the ground was white as far as we could see. It was minus eight degrees outside. We walked slowly, because the road was very icy.

Our hotel was a small family house near a lake. The owner was a friendly old man, who gave us hot tea and showed us our room. From the window, we could see tall trees covered in snow. On Sunday, it was time to go home. On the train, I looked at the white fields for the last time. I thought that winter is not always something to hate. In Hokkaido, people enjoy it. I hope to go back again one day.
