Today, millions of people wear glasses, and most of us never think about them. But for most of history, people with weak eyes had no help at all. Glasses are a fairly new invention.

The first glasses appeared in Italy about seven hundred years ago. We do not know the name of the inventor. Some people believe that he was a monk who wanted to read his books more easily. The first glasses were made of two round pieces of glass, held together by a piece of metal, and the user had to hold them in front of his nose. They helped only people who had trouble seeing things close to their eyes. This was a great help for older men who read and wrote for a living.

In the beginning, glasses were very expensive, and only rich people and scholars could buy them. But in the fifteenth century, printed books appeared in Europe. Suddenly, many more people wanted to read, and so many more people needed glasses. Makers began to produce them in large numbers, and the price came down.

For a long time, there were no arms on glasses. People had to hold them with their hands or put them on the nose. Then someone had the idea of a frame with two arms that rest on the ears. This design is much more comfortable, and it is still in use today.

Glasses for people who cannot see things far away came later. Today, we have many kinds: glasses for reading, for driving, and for working at a computer. Many people also wear small plastic lenses in their eyes, and some have an operation with a laser.

Glasses have changed the way we see the world, and not only in the simple sense. Because of them, scholars can work for many more years, and children with weak eyes can learn at school like everybody else.

Next time you put on your glasses in the morning, remember that for most of history, this small thing did not exist.
