For five years I took the bus to work every day. The bus was always full, and the trip took forty minutes. I usually arrived tired and in a bad mood. Last spring I decided to try something different. I started to walk.

At first, it was hard. My office is about five kilometres from my home, and I had not exercised for a long time. After the first week my legs hurt, and I almost gave up. But my neighbour told me, "Don't stop. The second week is always easier." She was right.

Walking has changed my mornings in many ways. I leave the house a little earlier, so I am never in a hurry. I pass a small park where old men play chess, and a bakery where I sometimes buy a warm roll. I listen to music or just enjoy the quiet. When I arrive at work, I feel awake and ready to start.

I also noticed some changes in my body. I sleep better at night, and I do not feel as tired in the afternoon. My doctor said that regular walking is good for the heart, and I believe it. I have also saved some money, because I do not buy a bus ticket every day.

Of course, walking is not perfect. On rainy days I still take the bus, and in summer it can be very hot. Some people live too far from work, and for them walking is not possible. But even a short walk can help. You could get off the bus one stop early, or walk to a shop instead of using the car.

I think everyone should try it for one month. You may find, like me, that the best part of the day is the trip itself.
