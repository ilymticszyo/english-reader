My grandmother was waiting for me at the bus stop. She looked a little older, but she still walked fast, and she still laughed a lot. "You are too thin," she said, and she took my bag. In her kitchen, there was already a big lunch on the table: soup, rice, vegetables from the garden, and fresh bread.

In the afternoon we walked around the farm. She has twelve hens, two cows, and a very old dog called Max. Early on Sunday morning, I woke up to the smell of coffee. We had breakfast together, and then it was time to go. She gave me a bag with eggs, apples, and a jar of honey. "Come back soon," she said. "The farm is better with you here."
