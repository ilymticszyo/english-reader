The Great Wall is one of the most famous buildings in the world. It goes over mountains, through deserts, and across grassland for thousands of kilometres. Many people think that it is one long wall, but this is not exactly true.

The first walls were built more than 2,500 years ago, during a time when China was divided into many small kingdoms. Each kingdom built walls to protect its land from its neighbours. About 2,200 years ago, a powerful ruler joined these kingdoms into one country. He ordered his workers to connect the old walls and make new ones in the north, to keep out the people who lived on the other side of the grassland.

Later rulers continued the work. The wall that most visitors see today was mostly built about five hundred years ago, in a time called the Ming period. The Ming builders used stone and strong bricks, and they made the wall high and wide enough for soldiers to walk on. Every few hundred metres, there was a tower where soldiers watched. When they saw an enemy, they made smoke or fire, and the news could travel along the wall very quickly.

Building the wall was very hard and dangerous. Thousands of workers, soldiers, and farmers carried stone up steep mountains with simple tools. Many of them died, and the wall has sad stories as well as great ones.

Did the wall really keep enemies away? Not always. Sometimes the enemies found a weak place or opened a gate with help from the inside. But the wall was also useful for other things. It was a road for moving soldiers and goods, and a way to control trade.

Today, the wall is a popular place for visitors. Some parts, near the capital, are crowded. Other parts are old and wild, with broken stones and tall grass. Many people say that these quiet parts are the most beautiful.

Walking on the wall, you can feel a long history. It reminds us of what people can build when they work together.
