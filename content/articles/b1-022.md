Every year, at the end of the winter holidays, many people in China and other countries in the east celebrate the Lantern Festival. It takes place in the middle of the first month of the new year, when the moon is full. It is one of the most beautiful nights of the year.

The main part of the festival is, of course, the lamps. In the days before the festival, people hang lights of all colours in the streets, in parks, and in front of their houses. Some are small and simple, made of red paper. Others are huge and take many weeks to build. You can see lamps in the shape of animals, flowers, boats, and even famous stories. When it gets dark, thousands of lights turn on together, and the whole city seems to shine.

Children love the festival. They walk through the streets with their own small lamps, often in the shape of rabbits or fish. Many of them carry a lamp that their parents made for them. In some places, there are also contests, and people try to find the answers to puzzles that are written on small pieces of paper.

There is special food, too. The most famous one is a small round ball made of rice flour, with a sweet filling inside. It is served in a hot soup. The round shape is a symbol of the full moon and of a family that stays together. Families usually eat it at home, after a big dinner.

Why do people celebrate this festival? There are many stories. One says that it began more than two thousand years ago, when an emperor asked people to light lamps in the temples. Another says that the lights helped people find their way home. Whatever the reason, the festival ends the winter and welcomes the spring.

If you visit China in February, try to find a night like this. Take a warm coat, bring a friend, and enjoy a night full of light.
