Every dentist gives the same advice: brush your teeth twice a day. It sounds easy, but many people do it badly or forget to do it. Why is it so important, and what is the right way?

Your mouth is full of tiny living things called germs. After you eat, especially sweet food, these germs make a soft, sticky layer on your teeth. If you do not remove it, it can make a hole in the tooth. Germs can also make your mouth sore, and in the end, you can lose teeth.

Good brushing stops this. You should brush in the morning and again before bed. The night is the most important time, because you make less saliva when you sleep, and your mouth cannot clean itself well. Use a soft brush and a small amount of toothpaste, which makes your teeth stronger.

Here is how to do it. Hold the brush at a small angle against your teeth. Move it in small, gentle circles. Do not push too hard. Clean the front, the back, and the top of each tooth. Do not forget your tongue, because germs live there, too. The whole job should take about two minutes. A song can help you to count the time.

Brushing is not enough alone. Teeth have small spaces between them, and the brush cannot reach them. Use a thin string made for this job once a day to clean these places.

Food is also important. Sweet drinks and sweets are bad for your teeth, especially if you have them often during the day. Try to have them only with meals. Water, milk, and cheese are much better.

Finally, visit a dentist every six months or once a year.

Good teeth are a gift for the whole of your life. The best time to start taking care of them is today.
