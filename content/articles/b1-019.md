Every person throws away a lot of rubbish every day. Some of it is food, but much of it is paper, plastic, glass, or metal. If we put these things in the right place, they can be used again. This is called recycling. It is not difficult, and you can start at home today.

The first step is to find out the rules in your area. Every city is a little different. In some places, you put everything in one bin, and in others, you need separate bins for paper, glass, and plastic. Look at the website of your city, or ask a neighbour.

The second step is to make it easy. Put two or three boxes in your kitchen and write on each one what it is for. When the boxes are easy to see and reach, everyone in the family will use them. Children enjoy this, and it is a good way to teach them to care about the planet.

The third step is to clean things before you put them in the bin. A bottle with old food in it can make other things dirty, and then the whole box cannot be used. A quick wash with water is usually enough. Take off the lids if the rules say so.

The fourth step is to learn what you cannot recycle. Some things look the same but are not. For example, paper with food on it, broken glasses, and many kinds of plastic bags often have to go in the normal rubbish. If you are not sure, it is better to ask than to guess.

Finally, remember that recycling is only one part of the answer. It is even better to make less rubbish at the start. You can take your own bag to the shop, buy only what you need, and repair things that are broken.

Small actions, repeated every day by millions of people, can make our world cleaner.
