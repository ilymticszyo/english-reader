My first day was not easy. When I walked in, thirty dogs started to bark at the same time. The noise was incredible. The manager, Rosa, took me around and showed me how to clean the cages and fill the bowls with food and water. The work is not beautiful. You have to be ready for bad smells and wet floors.

But the best part is walking the dogs. Each dog has its own character. There is a big, slow dog named Bruno, who walks like an old man and stops at every tree. After twenty minutes, she came and put her nose on my hand. I almost cried.

Many animals in the shelter have sad stories. Some were left by their owners, and others were found on the street. But Rosa says that the shelter is a place of hope. Every week, families come to meet the animals, and some of them find a new friend. Last Saturday, a young girl chose Pepper. When the girl hugged her, the little dog's tail moved from side to side for the first time since I knew her.

I do not think that I will ever be able to adopt every animal I meet. But I can give them a little time, food, and love. I also tell my friends and family about the shelter, and some of them come to help now.
