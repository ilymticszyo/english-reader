In many countries, people say that breakfast is the most important meal of the day. But what people eat in the morning is very different from one place to another. Let us take a short trip around the world.

In Britain, a traditional breakfast is big. It has eggs, bacon, sausages, beans, tomatoes, and toast. Today, most people eat it only at weekends or in a hotel. On a normal day, they have a bowl of cereal or just toast with tea.

In Japan, breakfast is often like a small dinner. A person may have rice, soup made from beans, a piece of fish, and some vegetables. Many families also eat a raw egg on top of the hot rice. It is a light, healthy meal, and it gives you energy for hours.

In Mexico, many people start the day with eggs and a hot sauce, often with tortillas and beans. In some places, there is also a thick, sweet drink made from corn. In Turkey, the table is full of small plates: cheese, olives, tomatoes, bread, and eggs, and everything is shared. People drink strong tea in small glasses and sit for a long time.

In France, breakfast is very small. Many people have only a cup of coffee and a piece of bread or a sweet roll called a croissant. In India, a popular breakfast in the south is a soft, round cake made from rice and beans, served with a hot soup and a cool white sauce.

In some countries, many people skip breakfast, and they say they are not hungry in the morning. Doctors say that this is not a problem for everyone. The most important thing is to eat food that is good for you, and to not eat too much sugar.
