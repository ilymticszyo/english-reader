In many countries, rice is the most important food. In my country, we eat it almost every day. But when I moved abroad to study, I discovered a problem. I did not know how to cook it! My rice was either hard or like soup. A friend from Korea finally taught me, and now I am a rice expert.

First, you must choose the right kind of rice. There are many kinds. Long rice is common in many countries, and it is light and dry. Short rice is softer and stickier, so it is good for special dishes. For everyday meals, any good rice will do, but you need to know which kind you have.

Second, wash the rice. Put it in a bowl, add cold water, and move it gently with your fingers. The water will turn white. Pour it out and do this again two or three times, until the water is almost clear.

Third, measure the water. This is the secret. For most kinds of rice, use about one and a half cups of water for each cup of rice. My Korean friend has another way: she puts a finger on top of the rice and fills the water up to the first line of her finger. It works every time.

Fourth, cook the rice. Put it in a pot with a tight lid, bring the water to the boil, and then turn the fire down very low. Now comes the most important rule: do not open the lid. If you do, the steam goes out, and the rice will not cook well. Wait for about fifteen minutes.

Finally, turn off the fire and leave the pot for ten more minutes. Then open the lid, and use a fork to gently mix the rice. It should be soft, with every grain separate.

Rice goes with almost everything: vegetables, fish, meat, or just an egg. And it reminds me of home, wherever I am.
