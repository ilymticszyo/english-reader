Twenty years ago, many cities were full of cars. The roads were crowded, the air was dirty, and it was dangerous to ride a bicycle. Today, things are starting to change. In many places, more and more people are going to work by bike.

Why is this happening? One reason is the cost. A car is expensive. You must pay for fuel, insurance, and a place to park. A bicycle costs much less, and you can keep it in your house. In a busy city, a bike is often faster, too. A car can sit in a long line of traffic, but a bike can go past it.

Another reason is health. Doctors say that adults should do some exercise every day, but many people have no time for the gym. If you ride to work, you can exercise and travel at the same time. Many riders say that they feel more awake and happier when they arrive at the office.

Cities also help. Some of them have built special roads only for bicycles, so riders feel safer. Some have also made it easy to borrow a bike. You take one from a stand in the street, ride to the place you want to go, and leave it at another stand.

Of course, cycling is not perfect. When it rains or snows, it is not much fun. Some people live too far from their work. And in cities with no bike roads, riding can still be dangerous.

But the idea is growing. Fewer cars mean cleaner air and less noise. Children can play outside more. Streets that were once for cars become places for people.

Maybe the best thing about the bicycle is how simple it is. It is cheap, quiet, and good for you. It is an old invention, but it may be one of the best answers to the problems of the modern city.
