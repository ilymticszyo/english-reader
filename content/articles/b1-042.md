Many people want to save money, but at the end of the month, there is nothing left. If this sounds like you, do not worry. Saving does not need a big salary. It needs a plan, and you can start with small steps.

First, find out where your money goes. For one month, write down everything that you buy, even a cup of coffee or a bus ticket. At the end, look at the list. Most people are surprised. They find that they spend a lot on things that they do not need, such as food delivery, subscriptions, or snacks.

Second, make a simple budget. Divide your money into three parts: things you must pay, such as rent, food, and bills; things you want, such as films and clothes; and savings. A popular rule is to use about half of your money for the first part, thirty percent for the second part, and twenty percent for savings. If that is too much, start with five or ten percent. Any amount is good.

Third, pay yourself first. On the day that you receive your salary, move your savings to a different account straight away. If the money is in the same account as your daily money, you will probably spend it. If you cannot see it, you will not miss it.

Next, make small changes. You can take lunch from home two days a week, walk instead of taking a taxi, or wait a few days before you buy something expensive. Often, after three days, you do not want it so much any more.

Last, give your savings a goal. It is easier to save when you have a reason, such as a holiday or a new computer.

Do not be sad if you make a mistake. The important thing is to keep trying. After a year, you will be happy that you started.
