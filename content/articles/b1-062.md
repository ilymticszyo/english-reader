When we are busy, we often eat whatever is nearest: a bag of crisps, a biscuit, or a sweet drink. These things are quick, but they are not good for us. They help us for a short time, and then we feel tired again. Here are some better ideas for snacks.

Fruit is the easiest choice. An apple, a banana, or some grapes is easy to carry and needs no preparation. Fruit has natural sugar, and it makes you feel full. If you take a piece of fruit to work, you will not need to buy a snack from a machine.

Yoghurt is good for your body, too. Buy plain yoghurt, because many fruit yoghurts have a lot of sugar. You can add a little honey or some pieces of fresh fruit to make it sweeter. A small bowl is a perfect snack in the afternoon.

Vegetables can be fun if you prepare them in a nice way. Cut some carrots, cucumbers, or peppers into sticks and put them in a box.

Another simple idea is a slice of brown bread with a little cheese or peanut butter. It will give you slow, steady energy. Boiled eggs are also very good.

A few more tips can help. Do not wait until you are very hungry, because then you will choose whatever is quick. Keep healthy food in your bag or on your desk. Drink water, too. Sometimes, when we think we are hungry, we are really only thirsty.

You do not have to give up the foods that you love. Just make the healthy choice a little easier than the other one.
