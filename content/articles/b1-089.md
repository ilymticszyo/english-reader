Packing for a short trip can be stressful. You want to bring everything you need, but you do not want a heavy bag. Here are some simple tips that help me every time.

First, make a list a few days before you leave. Write down everything you think you will need. Then read the list again and take away some things. Most people bring too much.

Next, check the weather where you are going. If it will be cold, bring a warm jacket and a few thin layers. Thin layers are better than one thick coat, because you can take them off when you are warm. If it will rain, a small umbrella is enough.

Choose clothes that you can mix and match. For a three-day trip, you do not need a different outfit for every day. Two pairs of trousers and three shirts can make many combinations. Bring clothes in similar colours, and they will all go together.

Roll your clothes instead of folding them. Rolled clothes take less space, and they get fewer lines. Put your shoes at the bottom of the bag, and fill them with socks. Keep heavy things near the wheels or the back.

Put small items in a clear bag. This includes your toothbrush, medicine, and chargers. You can find them quickly, and nothing will get lost.

Finally, keep your important things in a bag that you carry with you. Your ticket, money, passport, and phone should never go in a suitcase.

The night before, look at your list one last time. Then close the bag and relax. A good trip begins with a calm mind.
