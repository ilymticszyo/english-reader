When I started learning English, I found grammar books boring. I could remember the rules for one day, and then I forgot them. Then a friend gave me some advice: "Listen to songs, and read the words." I tried it, and I think it is one of the best ways to learn.

First, songs are fun. When you like a song, you want to hear it again and again. This is very good for learning, because you meet the same words many times without trying. After a week, you know the words of the chorus. After a month, you can sing the whole song.

Second, songs help with sounds. In English, the way we say a word is often not the way we write it. In a song, the singer says each word clearly, and the music gives you the rhythm. You can sing along, and your mouth learns the movements. This is also a good way to practise speaking if you feel shy in front of other people.

Third, songs teach you real English. The words are the same as the words people use in daily life: love, home, friends, time, or "I miss you". Some of the sentences are not perfect grammar, but they show how people really talk.

There are some problems. Some singers go too fast, and some songs use strange words. If you cannot understand a line, do not worry. You can look for the words on the internet, find the meaning in a dictionary, and then listen again. Choose slow songs at the beginning, and then try faster ones.

Here is my plan. Every week I choose one song. On Monday I listen to it. On Tuesday I read the words. On Wednesday I look up new words. On Thursday and Friday I sing it. At the weekend I try to write the story of the song in my own words.

It sounds simple, and it is. But after one year, I know about two hundred new words, and I enjoy every minute.
