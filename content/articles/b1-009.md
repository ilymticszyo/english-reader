After water, tea is the most popular drink in the world. People drink it in hot countries and cold countries, in big cities and small villages. But where does tea come from, and why do so many people love it?

Tea comes from the leaves of a plant that first grew in southern China. A famous old story says that, about five thousand years ago, some leaves fell into a cup of hot water for an emperor. He liked the taste, and people began to drink tea. Nobody knows if the story is true, but we do know that people in China drank tea a very long time ago.

At first, tea was a kind of medicine. Later, it became an everyday drink. Travellers took it to Japan, India, and the Middle East. Around the year 1600, ships brought it to Europe. It was very expensive at that time, so only rich families could buy it.

Today, every country has its own way of drinking tea. In Britain, many people like strong black tea with milk, and some have a small meal with it in the afternoon. In Japan, people often drink green tea, and a traditional tea ceremony can take more than an hour. In Morocco, people make tea with fresh green leaves and a lot of sugar, and they pour it from a high place to make bubbles. In India, many people buy hot, sweet tea from small street shops.

Why is tea so popular? One reason is that it is cheap and easy to make. Another is that it is not as strong as coffee, so many people can drink it in the evening. But maybe the best reason is a feeling. When we give someone a cup of tea, we say, "Sit down, relax, and talk to me."

In every country, a cup of tea is a small moment of peace.
