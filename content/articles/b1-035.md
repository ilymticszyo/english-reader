Every night before I go to bed, I write in my diary for ten minutes. I have done this for six years, and I think it is one of the best habits that I have.

I started when I was in high school. My teacher said that writing every day would improve my English, so I bought a small notebook. At first, I wrote only a few sentences, and they were boring. "I got up. I went to school. I ate rice." But after a few weeks, I began to write more about my thoughts and feelings.

The first good thing about a diary is that it helps you to think. When you are worried or angry, it can be hard to understand why. If you write about it, the problem becomes clearer. Often, when I finish writing, I feel lighter, and I know what to do next.

The second good thing is memory. Our lives are busy, and we forget a lot. Last month, I read my diary from three years ago and found a story that I had completely forgotten. I laughed for a long time. It is like a small time machine.

The third good thing is that it makes my writing better. Nobody will read my diary, so I do not worry about mistakes. I can try new words and long sentences without fear. Over the years, this has made me more confident in English.

Some people say that they have no time, or that nothing interesting happens to them. You do not need to write a lot. Three sentences are enough. You can write about the weather, a conversation, or one thing that made you smile. Small things are often the most interesting after a few years.

If you want to start, here is my advice. Choose a time that is the same every day. Keep the notebook near your bed. And do not try to be perfect. A diary is only for you.
