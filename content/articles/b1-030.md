When you learn a language, you need many words. A good vocabulary helps you to read, to listen, and to speak with more confidence. But it is not easy to remember so many new things. Here are five ways that can help.

The first way is to learn words in groups, not alone. If you only learn the word "apple", it is easy to forget it. But if you learn it with "eat an apple", "a red apple", and "apple juice", it is much easier to remember. Your brain likes to find connections.

The second way is to use the words as soon as you can. After you learn a new word, write three sentences with it. Say them aloud. Try to use the word in a conversation the next day. When you use a word for real, it stays in your memory for a long time.

The third way is to review often. Scientists say that we forget most new things in the first few days. So you should look at a word again after one day, then after three days, then after a week, and then after a month. You can make small paper cards, with the word on one side and the meaning on the other.

The fourth way is to read and listen to things that you enjoy. If you like football, read about football. If you like cooking, watch videos about cooking. When the topic is interesting, you pay more attention, and you learn words without feeling that you are working.

The fifth way is to use pictures and stories. If you see a picture in your mind, you can remember a word better. For example, to remember the word "tired", imagine a person who is sleeping on a desk.

Ten new words every week is only a small number, but after a year, you will know more than five hundred.
