A paper plane is easy to make, and it is a lot of fun. You only need one sheet of paper. Here is a simple way to make a plane that flies far.

First, put the paper on a table. The long side should be in front of you. Fold the paper in half from top to bottom. Then open it again. You should see a line in the middle.

Next, fold the two top corners to the middle line. Now the top of the paper looks like a roof. Press the folds with your fingers so they stay flat.

Then fold the new edges to the middle line again. The paper now looks long and thin, with a sharp point at the front. Fold the whole thing in half along the first line. The folds should be on the outside.

Now make the wings. Fold one side down so that the edge is level with the bottom of the plane. Turn the plane over and do the same on the other side. The two wings should look the same.

Hold the plane under the middle and throw it gently. Do not throw it too hard. A strong throw can make the plane turn over in the air.

If your plane goes down too fast, bend the back edge of the wings up a little. If it goes up and then falls, bend the back edge down a little. Small changes can make a big difference.

Try making planes with different shapes. Some are good for long flights, and some are better for tricks. You can also have a contest with your friends and see whose plane goes the furthest.

Many teachers use paper planes to talk about how real planes fly. It is a good way to learn, and nobody has to sit still. Try it on a quiet afternoon. You may be surprised by how much you enjoy it.
