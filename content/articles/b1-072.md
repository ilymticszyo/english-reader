Almost everyone feels nervous before an important test. Your heart beats fast, your hands feel cold, and your mind goes blank. A little stress can be useful, because it helps you to pay attention. But too much can make it hard to think. Here are some ways to feel calmer.

Start early. The best way to feel calm is to be well prepared. Do not wait until the night before the test. Make a plan and study a little every day. Break the work into small parts, and tick each one off when you finish it. This will give you a feeling of progress.

Take care of your body. Sleep is very important, because a tired brain does not work well. Try to go to bed at the same time each night, and do not study until very late. Eat a good breakfast on the day of the test, and drink water.

Practise breathing. When you feel afraid, you often breathe fast and high in your chest. This makes you feel even more nervous. Slow your breathing down. Breathe in through your nose for four seconds, hold it for a moment, and then breathe out slowly for six seconds. Do this five times. You will feel your body relax.

Think positive thoughts. Many students say to themselves, "I will fail," and this makes things worse. Try to change these thoughts. Say, "I have worked hard, and I will do my best." It may feel strange at first, but it really helps.

On the day, arrive a little early, so that you do not have to hurry. When you get the test, read all the questions first. Start with the easy ones, because they will give you confidence. If you do not know an answer, move on and come back later. Watch the clock, but do not look at it every minute.

Remember that one test does not decide your future. If it does not go well, you can try again. What matters is that you did your best.
