When the weather is cold, there is nothing better than a bowl of hot soup. It is cheap, it is easy to make, and it is good for you. You do not need special tools or a lot of experience. Here is a simple way to make vegetable soup for four people.

First, buy your vegetables. You will need two big potatoes, two carrots, one onion, and a few other things that you like. Tomatoes, beans, and green leaves are all good. It is a good idea to use whatever you already have at home. Soup is a great way to use things before they go bad.

Second, wash and cut everything. Take off the skin of the potatoes and carrots, and cut them into small pieces. Small pieces cook faster, and they are easier to eat. Cut the onion into thin pieces too.

Third, cook the onion first. Put a little oil in a big pot and heat it on a low fire. Add the onion and cook it for about five minutes, until it is soft and a little yellow. Stir it often so that it does not turn black. This gives the soup a sweet, rich taste.

Now add the other vegetables and stir for two minutes. Then pour in about one litre of water, and add some salt. When the water begins to boil, turn the fire down, cover the pot, and wait for twenty-five minutes. The vegetables are ready when you can push a fork through them easily.

At this point, you have a choice. You can eat the soup just as it is, with the pieces of vegetables. Or you can push some of the vegetables against the side of the pot with a big spoon, which makes the soup thicker.

Taste the soup, and add more salt or some black pepper if you want. Serve it hot, with a piece of bread. Any soup that is left will be even better tomorrow.
