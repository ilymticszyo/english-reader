In my family, Sunday morning means pancakes. My father started this habit when I was a child, and now I make them for my own friends. They are easy to make, and you do not need a lot of things.

First, you need the right things. For about six pancakes, take one cup of flour, one egg, one cup of milk, a spoon of sugar, and a little salt. You also need some butter and a pan.

Put the flour, the sugar, and the salt in a big bowl. Make a small hole in the middle and break the egg into it. Then add the milk slowly. Mix everything with a spoon, but not too much. When it looks like thick cream, leave it for ten minutes.

Now heat the pan on a medium fire. Put in a small piece of butter, and when it is hot, pour in some of the mixture. A small cup is a good way to measure the right amount. Wait for about two minutes. When you see small bubbles on the top, it is time to turn it over. Use a wide knife or a flat spoon, and try to be quick. Cook the other side for one more minute.

The first pancake is often not very good. It can be too dark or too thick, because the pan is not ready.

Put the pancakes on a warm plate and cover them, so that they stay warm while you cook the rest. Then bring them to the table with fruit, honey, or jam.

Cooking together is the best part. One person mixes, another turns the pancakes, and a third person makes the tea. The kitchen smells wonderful, and everyone is in a good mood.

If you have never made pancakes, try this on your next free morning. You will see that a simple breakfast can make the whole day feel special.
